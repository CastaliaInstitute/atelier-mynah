Myndfulness @ Home

A cell-free low-dopamine home assistant.

Mynah gives the house a calmer sense of time: ambient glow, voice when invited, music without the scroll, and a bridge into Castalia's instruments for inquiry and learning.

Explore the build Source on GitHub

Mostly quiet, useful when invited.

Mynah starts from domestic attention rather than phone attention. Its default behavior is a full-screen time-of-day glow with conservative night brightness, slow transitions, and just enough status to keep a room oriented without opening a feed.

Dawn

A slow lift toward morning: rose, peach, and a little cool sky.

Day

Neutral presence, often nearly off, so the object can fade into the room.

Dusk

Warm wind-down cues with brightness falling before contrast starts to feel sharp.

Sleep

Orientation-only nightlight levels for nurseries, kitchens, and bedside tables.

A home assistant without the dopamine loop.

Mynah can coordinate voice, rooms, music, LAN discovery, and pocket satellites, but the product posture stays cell-free: no social feed, no notification casino, no pocket rectangle asking to be checked again.

Home glow

Android ambient mode keeps the mini-phone form factor readable as a quiet table, shelf, or outlet-side object.

Invited voice

Shared Edge Functions route short voice turns, speech-to-text, faculty-flavored replies, and TTS without turning conversation into another attention surface.

Rooms and rhythm

Spotify and LAN/Home Assistant work make Mynah a small coordination point for household audio and status, not a general-purpose phone replacement.

The home base for Castalia's hardware ecology.

Mynah is the cell-free household node. Astrolabe is its round pocket satellite for questions, time, moon, memory, and room presence. Atlas provides purpose-built learning compute around the same home.

Designed for ordinary places.

The main hardware expression stays phone-shaped: portrait rectangle, one dominant glowing face, thin enough for pockets and docks. The images stay grounded in real surfaces rather than imaginary product silhouettes.

Current build map

From ambient phone to pocket satellite.

The Android app is the primary home surface. Supabase functions provide shared voice and faculty routes. Astrolabe/Pocketwatch is the round satellite path for the home, while Atlas names the larger learning-compute family around it.
