A day in the life

Computing that knows when to disappear.

Mynah is a family of quiet physical and conversational interfaces into the same living context: person, relationship, place, time, physiology, inquiry, memory, permission and action.

Ring makes presence ambient. Room makes the environment responsive. Person carries private intent. Alpheus gives the whole system a voice.

5:45 AM

The day begins before anyone is awake.

The Mynah Ring has been sensing through the night: sleep timing, movement, heart rate, HRV, temperature trends and recovery signals. It records physiology without pretending to know what a person is thinking.

SleepHRVTemperatureMovement

For a menstruating family member, opt-in cycle awareness can combine personally entered cycle dates and symptoms with temperature, sleep and recovery trends. Cycle data remains private by default and is never treated as a deterministic explanation for mood, behavior or relationship dynamics.

6:25 AM

Astrolabe senses nearness.

You walk toward Astrolabe. You do not touch it. RSSI from your Ring rises and the ambient astronomical face becomes your personal LunaSay context.

Far away → ambient. Nearby → personal. Pause → detail. Act only when you choose.

LunaSay can combine time, calendar, astronomy, sleep and recovery trends, menstrual-cycle context where explicitly enabled, and relationship context into a glanceable orientation.

It can also surface family synastry: symbolic relationship geometry among partners, parents and children. It might suggest that family members are moving at different tempos today or that a quieter shared window appears later. Synastry is a reflective language for timing and relationship, not a prediction engine.

6:40 AM

Person becomes the private action layer.

You pick up Mynah Person, the deliberately small device that travels with you. Ring proximity helps establish identity and private context.

“Alpheus, morning.”

Alpheus is Castalia's sovereign AI. Through Person, the conversation defaults personal and private. Through Room, it defaults shared and ambient.

6:45 AM

Mynah Room wakes the place.

Room belongs to the physical space. Kitchen lights rise. Quiet music begins. Atlas wakes. The printer starts.

Person: What do I want to do right now?
Room: What should this room be doing?
Alpheus: What does the context mean, and what should happen next?

6:50 AM

The morning edition comes off the printer.

The Gazette is finite household and world orientation: weather, family schedule, sunrise and sunset, astronomical notes, a few relevant news items and things the family explicitly asked to remember.

The Gazetteer is about place: what is flowering, migrating, flowing, changing or historically interesting nearby. It ends with something the family can go investigate.

Today's inquiry: Where could a frog find water near us?

Paper becomes another Mynah interface. There is no endless feed underneath the page.

7:15 AM

The kids compose an inquiry with iNQ Coins.

The children choose from a collection of bamboo inquiry coins. Each coin is one concept rendered as a black sumi-e glyph.

iNQ Coin · 35 mm diameter · 1.3 mm thick · bamboo · sumi-e glyph · NFC

One child chooses FROG. Another chooses WATER. They place both on Atlas.

Frog + Water → Where could a frog find water here today?

The coins are not flash cards. They form a tangible language for combining ideas.

7:30 AM

Alpheus answers, then gives the question back.

A child asks the room, “Alpheus, do frogs sleep?” Alpheus answers briefly, then returns the inquiry to Atlas: where might a frog rest, and how could the children find evidence?

Sometimes the most useful AI answer is a better question.

8:00 AM

Atlas goes into the world.

Atlas is a Samsung Galaxy A16 in a protective field case with a built-in specimen-tray lid. The lid stores and presents iNQ Coins and can hold small field finds while the phone's cameras document them.

The children carry Atlas, selected iNQ Coins and the Gazetteer outside. WATER can mark the active inquiry. Add INSECT and the inquiry changes again.

Water + Insect → Would insects here make this a better place for frogs?
8:20 AM

Atlas teaches how to know.

The children point Atlas toward a plant. It may suggest an identification, but the important part is the evidence: leaf shape, stem, flower or fruit, growth habit and habitat.

The lesson is not that the AI knows. The lesson is how a naturalist could know.

A child sees a beetle and says, “It went underneath the log.” Atlas preserves observer, place, time, evidence, confidence and the active iNQ context. It can remain Unknown beetle #3.

8:30 AM

The parent stays present without hovering.

An adult Ring and Person establish that an authorized adult is nearby. Atlas does not require continuous surveillance video.

Atlas requests adult help: berry identification.

The parent joins only when needed.

9:00 AM

Person captures a thought through Alpheus.

“Alpheus, remember this: compare this year's frog observations with last spring.”

No app, keyboard or filing decision. Because the request came through Person, it defaults private unless explicitly shared.

10:00 AM

eInq becomes the deliberate surface.

eInq receives fragments from Person, Atlas observations and questions sparked by the Gazette and Gazetteer. It is where you read, annotate, compare sources and develop ideas.

Person: don't lose this.
eInq: think about this.
Atlas: investigate this.
Room: make the place respond.
Alpheus: reason and converse across them.

11:30 AM

A difficult moment becomes protected context.

After a disagreement with a spouse, you privately tell Person:

“Alpheus, save for MARIA: I think I went straight into fixing when listening was needed.”

This becomes private Theritor/MARIA context. A spouse can independently preserve another perspective. Neither note automatically becomes shared.

12:00 PM

The household changes state.

Lunch begins. Astrolabe knows there is nothing urgent. Person quiets nonessential interruptions. Room changes the environment. Atlas docks. eInq closes. The printed Gazette remains on the table.

A useful Mynah metric: How much useful computing happened today without requiring someone to stare at a screen?
1:15 PM

The same Astrolabe recognizes another person.

A spouse approaches. Their Ring signal rises and the face becomes their LunaSay context without exposing anyone else's private physiology, cycle information or private notes.

If they have enabled cycle awareness, LunaSay may quietly show a personal note such as an expected phase transition or a pattern they themselves have chosen to track. That information does not become family context merely because it exists.

1:30 PM

The kids build another question by hand.

FROG returns. Add NIGHT. Then SOUND.

Frog + Night + Sound → What do frogs do after dark, and what might we hear?

Atlas can suggest an evening listening activity. Inquiry becomes something children physically compose.

2:30 PM

The devices separate by role.

Ring comes because it belongs to the body. Person comes because it captures, controls and gives private access to Alpheus. Atlas comes if the outing is exploratory. Selected iNQ Coins travel in the specimen-tray lid. Room stays because Room belongs to the place.

3:30 PM

Atlas separates observation from natural range.

At a zoo, a child observes a zebra. Atlas records a captive observation while keeping the animal's natural range separate. A map is not the territory, an image is not a specimen, recognition is not certainty, and classification is not understanding.

4:30 PM

Room recognizes the family returning.

Rings and Person devices reappear. Room infers who is likely home and lets the environment respond to people rather than anonymous motion.

“Alpheus, continue the music in the kitchen.” Shared voice stays with Room; personal voice stays with Person.

6:00 PM

LunaSay keeps different kinds of evidence separate.

Passing Astrolabe, a family member may see sleep and recovery context, their own menstrual-cycle context if enabled, and symbolic family synastry. These are distinct lenses.

Physiology describes measured or entered bodily context. Cycle awareness tracks a personal biological rhythm. Synastry supplies symbolic relationship language. None is allowed to silently become a diagnosis of mood, personality or marriage.

6:30 PM

Dinner is allowed to be dinner.

Room handles lighting and music. Person remains available. Alpheus is available through both but silent unless invited. Atlas is docked. eInq is closed. Rings continue quietly.

7:30 PM

The iNQ Coins return for reflection.

Atlas offers memories from the day. The child selects a bamboo BEETLE coin and places it on Atlas. Their own voice returns: “It went underneath the log.” Add HOME and Atlas asks what makes the underside of a log a good home.

No streak. No badge. No points. Curiosity itself is sufficient.

8:00 PM

Theritor meets the marriage through MARIA.

Theritor is the therapist/editor system with access to the source material for the author's books. MARIA is its marital-therapy persona. Room quiets the space; eInq becomes the shared surface; Rings establish presence.

Private memory → explicit permission → shared therapeutic space.
8:10 PM

Body rhythms become context, never ammunition.

If both partners explicitly permit it, MARIA may note relevant shared context such as poor sleep. Menstrual-cycle information remains owned by the person it describes and is only introduced if that person chooses.

MARIA might ask whether fatigue, pain, or a personally reported cycle-related experience feels relevant. She does not attribute conflict to “hormones,” and a spouse never receives cycle data merely because MARIA can access it.

8:20 PM

Theritor reads life against the books.

Theritor can place three things beside one another: what a person wrote, how that person says they live, and how a spouse experiences them. The books become mirrors, not weapons.

8:30 PM

Family synastry enters as another lens.

With mutual consent, MARIA can invite a LunaSay reading of the couple or broader family synastry. It is presented as symbolic language and metaphor rather than causal explanation.

Ring — physiological evidence
Cycle awareness — private personal biological rhythm
Theritor — authored and self-descriptive evidence
Relationship memory — shared lived history
LunaSay — astrology, synastry and symbolic timing
MARIA — therapeutic dialogue and synthesis

8:40 PM

MARIA makes the pattern visible.

A recurring loop can appear on eInq: concern → solution proposed → insufficient acknowledgment → solution feels rejected → frustration.

Where can either person change the sequence?

A small experiment emerges: before solving, ask whether the other person wants listening or solutions.

9:30 PM

The parent's inquiry becomes tomorrow's child inquiry.

Back on eInq, something from the children's day still invites attention. A parent reads further, then tells Person:

“Alpheus, send this to Atlas tomorrow: Why does this plant grow here?”
10:30 PM

Astrolabe closes the day without touch.

You approach. Ring RSSI rises. Astrolabe becomes personal again and offers a few things worth remembering, tomorrow's inquiry, and perhaps a gentle recovery or cycle note if you chose to track it.

No productivity score. No moral judgment. No demand to optimize.

11:00 PM

Rest.

Room puts the house into night state. Atlas sleeps. eInq closes. Person charges. Astrolabe returns to ambient astronomy. The Ring remains on the hand.

The ecosystem

Each interface has one clear job.

Presence

Mynah Ring

Who is here, and what is the body doing? Sleep, movement, recovery, temperature trends, identity and nearness.

Orientation

Astrolabe / LunaSay

Where am I in time, and what context matters now? Astronomy, personal rhythms, opt-in cycle awareness, astrology, family synastry and reflection.

Intent

Mynah Person

What do I want to do right now? Private Alpheus voice, capture, command, communication, control and quick identification.

Place

Mynah Room

What should this room be doing? Shared voice, lights, music, local devices, printer and ambient household intelligence.

Sovereign AI

Alpheus

What does the context mean? Castalia's sovereign conversational intelligence, available through Room and Person.

Thought

Mynah eInq

What deserves sustained attention? Reading, annotation, synthesis, writing and deliberate inquiry.

Inquiry

Atlas

What is this, and how can I find out? Samsung A16 field device with specimen-tray lid for child-centered exploration and observation.

Tangible language

iNQ Coins

35 mm × 1.3 mm bamboo NFC coins with sumi-e glyphs, physically combined to form questions and concepts.

Print

Gazette / Gazetteer

Finite daily orientation on paper: one for the household and world, one for the place outside the door.

Relationship

Theritor / MARIA

How do what we have written, lived, felt and remembered illuminate how we live together?
