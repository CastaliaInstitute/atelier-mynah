A day in the life

Computing that knows when to disappear.

Mynah moves intelligence out of apps and into the rhythms of family life: presence that is ambient, a morning paper that ends, wooden coins children use to ask questions, and devices that recede when their work is done.

Most of the day, most of the devices should be quiet.

Morning

Before the screens.

The first interactions are almost invisible: a ring senses, an Astrolabe notices who is near, a room wakes, and a finite morning edition appears on paper.

5:45 AM

The day begins before anyone touches a screen.

The Mynah Ring has been sensing sleep, movement, heart rate, HRV and temperature through the night. Nothing demands attention. The data simply becomes context.

SleepHRVTemperatureMovement
6:25 AM

Astrolabe notices that you are near.

RSSI from the Ring rises and the ambient astronomical face becomes your LunaSay face. No tap is required.

Far away → ambient. Nearby → personal. Pause → detail.

LunaSay gives orientation rather than notifications: time, weather, calendar, astronomy and the personal rhythms you choose to track.

6:40 AM

A private voice travels with you.

You pick up Mynah Person and press the side button.

“Alpheus, morning.”

Person is the private mobile voice into Alpheus, Castalia's sovereign AI: good for capture, commands and short conversations; deliberately bad at becoming somewhere to disappear for an hour.

6:45 AM

The kitchen wakes around you.

Mynah Room raises the lights, starts quiet music and wakes the Atlas station. Then the printer starts moving. No dashboard appears.

6:50 AM

The morning edition arrives on paper.

The Gazette says what matters today: weather, schedule, sunrise and sunset, a few relevant stories and things the household asked to remember.

The Gazetteer says what is happening here: what is flowering, migrating, flowing, changing or worth noticing nearby.

Gazette = today. Gazetteer = here. The paper ends; there is no feed underneath it.

Inquiry

Children ask with their hands.

The most distinctive Mynah interaction is deliberately physical: pre-literate children combine small bamboo iNQ Coins into questions that Atlas can help them investigate in the real world.

iNQ Coins

A tangible inquiry language.

35 mm diameter · 1.3 mm thick · bamboo · sumi-e glyph · NFC

The glyphs on production coins are sumi-e brush drawings; the diagram here represents the same three concepts schematically.

Frog + Water → Where could a frog find water here today?
7:15 AM

The children build a question.

One child chooses FROG. Another chooses WATER. They place the coins on Atlas. Add INSECT and the question changes again.

Water + Insect → Would insects make this a better place for frogs?

The coins are not flash cards. They let children combine ideas before they need to read or type.

7:30 AM

Sometimes Alpheus answers with another question.

A child asks, “Alpheus, do frogs sleep?” Alpheus answers briefly and hands the curiosity back to Atlas.

Where do you think a frog would choose to rest?

The goal is not to keep the child talking to the AI. It is to send the child back toward the world.

Atlas

A Samsung Galaxy A16 built for field inquiry.

Atlas lives in a protective field case with a specimen-tray lid. The tray carries the day's iNQ Coins and can hold appropriate finds such as stones, fallen leaves, shells and seed pods while the cameras document what the child sees.

⌖Place and time are automatic.
Observations become part of a geographic memory.
◉Identification shows evidence.
Leaf shape, stem, fruit, flower, habitat—not merely a name.
?Uncertainty is preserved.
Atlas is allowed to say “I don't know yet.”
8:20 AM

Atlas teaches how to know.

A child points Atlas toward a plant. It may suggest an identification, but it also points out the evidence. A beetle disappears under a log; the child records what happened. It can remain Unknown beetle #3 for as long as necessary.

8:30 AM

The parent is available without hovering.

An adult's Ring and Person establish that help is nearby. Safety-sensitive questions can escalate to the parent; otherwise, the inquiry remains the child's.

Atlas requests adult help: berry identification.

Adult attention

Capture quickly. Think slowly.

Person is for not losing the thought. eInq is for deciding whether the thought deserves any more of your life.

9:00 AM

A thought disappears into Person instead of a phone.

“Alpheus, remember this: compare this year's frog observations with last spring.”

No app, keyboard or inbox. Person captures intent while it is still small.

10:00 AM

Later, eInq is where thought slows down.

At a desk, eInq receives only the fragments that actually deserve attention: the frog note, the children's observations, perhaps something from the Gazette.

Person captures. eInq considers. Atlas investigates.

Family rhythm

Context changes with the person.

Intimate context belongs first to the individual. The same shared object can behave differently as different people approach it.

12:00 PM

Lunch is allowed to be lunch.

Room quiets. Atlas docks. eInq closes. Nobody needs to put the household into “family mode.” The context has already changed.

How much useful computing happened today without requiring anyone to stare at a screen?
1:15 PM

Astrolabe changes with whoever approaches.

A spouse approaches and their Ring signal rises. Astrolabe becomes their LunaSay face without exposing anyone else's notes or physiology.

If they choose to track menstrual-cycle information, it can appear here alongside sleep, temperature and calendar context. It remains theirs unless they deliberately bring it into another context.

Family synastry

A symbolic language for rhythm.

LunaSay can offer a light family-synastry reading: perhaps one parent is better suited to focused work this morning, another to a slower start, while the children's charts suggest a more outward afternoon.

It does not tell anyone how they will behave. It gives the family another language for noticing timing, difference and relationship.

1:30 PM

The children change the question.

FROG returns. Then NIGHT. Then SOUND.

Frog + Night + Sound → What might we hear after dark?

Three wooden coins have turned into a new investigation.

3:30 PM

Atlas keeps observation and reality separate.

At a zoo, a child can record where a zebra was observed while keeping that location distinct from its natural range. A map is not the territory. Recognition is not certainty.

Evening

The devices recede again.

Reflection is brief, family time remains mostly non-computational, and the most intimate tools appear only when deliberately invited.

5:15 PM

The day comes back together without becoming a feed.

Person has a few voice fragments. Atlas has observations. eInq offers what might deserve a second look. Most of it can disappear.

Capture should be easy. Accumulation should be optional.

6:30 PM

Dinner is mostly non-computational.

Room handles light and music. Alpheus is available if invited, but quiet otherwise. The devices have done enough.

7:30 PM

The children choose what to remember.

Atlas shows a few moments from the day. A child places the beetle coin on the tray and hears their own observation again.

Which one do you want to remember?

No streak. No badge. Curiosity itself is sufficient.

8:15 PM

MARIA is invited, not ambient.

Theritor's MARIA persona can support a deliberate relationship session. Private notes remain private until their owner brings them in. Ring physiology can be context, authored work can be a mirror, and LunaSay's synastry can be used as a symbolic lens—each kept distinct.

Measurement, interpretation, symbolism and reflection are different kinds of evidence.
10:30 PM

Astrolabe closes the day without touch.

You approach. Ring RSSI rises. LunaSay offers a few things worth remembering and perhaps a gentle note about rest. Then you move away and the personal face disappears.

Underneath

Mynah Context

Person · relationship · place · time · physiology · object · inquiry · observation · source · memory · permission · action

All Mynah devices can understand shared context. None automatically gains permission to cross a person's private boundary merely because the information exists elsewhere in the system.

Exist → Approach → Perceive → Pause → Act only if necessary.
Context may flow easily across devices. It must cross human boundaries deliberately.
